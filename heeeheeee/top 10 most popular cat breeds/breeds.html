Top 10 Cat Breeds 🐱

Meet ten of the most popular and beloved cat breeds.

📏 Medium ❤️ Outgoing 🧼 Special Care

Scottish Folds are recognizable by their distinctive folded ears and sweet personalities.

📏 Medium ❤️ Sweet 🧶 Medium Grooming
